About

I’ve been at Bell since 2022, working on the automation behind the datacenter and cloud infrastructure that carries Canada’s 5G network. I started as a Developer I building Terraform modules for firewalls on AWS and GCP, and moved up to Developer II, where build zero-touch provisioning software for the physical routers, firewalls and load balancers (Cisco, Juniper, F5 and Palo Alto) inside Bell's global Datacenters nationwide.

What I care about most is software that keeps working when something fails. That is why I spend so much time on failure-recovery paths, self-healing behaviour, test suites and boring, repeatable deployments. I’ve also been exploring AI-assisted operations: Gemini on Vertex AI and MCP servers that hand on-call engineers the telemetry they need in the first minutes of an incident.

I enjoy teaching what I know. I mentor newer developers, run training workshops for engineering teams, support SRE on-call, and was a teaching assistant for software engineering at Carleton. I’m looking for backend, platform or infrastructure software roles where reliability and automation are the product. If that sounds like your team, get in touch.
